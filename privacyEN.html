Privacy Policy

Beagle.Chat

There are many privacy and security related concerns and problems with today's most used instant messenger and social network software:

  • Security: the platform or the hackers can get the call log or even the content.
  • Privacy: a lot of spam advertising information.
  • Interconnection: mostly no communication between platforms.

When it comes to trading and business it becomes especially important. Just as an oracle plays an important role in the DeFi ecosystem, cross-chain messaging and real-time communication will be more critical. In the blockchain community we still use centralized messaging and social networks, such as Twitter and Facebook, to communicate. It should be changed.

What this project is

This project is to build a real-time meeting and chat application based on Web3 technologies and infrastructure found and available in most blockchain projects, such as peer-to-peer networks, smart contracts and ENS (Ethereum Name Service). Beagle.chat will serve the blockchain community as a cross-chain instant messaging platform. It is a real-time communication system with most of the features found in today's instant messengers.

No registration is needed before using it. Anyone with a wallet address, an ENS name or any DID can make use of it just by signing with the private key that belongs only to them. It will not be owned or controlled by any person or organization.

The goals of this project

  • No registration, no account name and password; a DID and a signature are the way to authenticate and authorize.
  • No server, no database super user; the service is powered by nodes run by miners, and all data is encrypted.
  • No spam.
